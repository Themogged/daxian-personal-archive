01 / LETTER

마음에 남는 순간들

For the moments
that stay.

Daxian,

Some moments seem small while they are happening, but later they stay with us in a way that is impossible to forget. A look, a laugh, a song, or simply being close can mean more than we first realize.

I love how easy it is to appreciate those moments with you. They do not have to be perfect or extraordinary; it is enough that they are ours. Thank you for making ordinary days feel soft and beautiful.

There are feelings I cannot always explain completely, but they are here: quiet, sincere, and present. I wanted to leave you these words so you know that every moment we share has a special place in my heart.

This is with love, straight from me to you; simply, it is my heart.

avec douceur, pour toi.
