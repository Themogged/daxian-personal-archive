A LETTER / FOR DAXIAN

마음을 담아, Daxian에게.

For the moments
that stay.

Daxian,

Some moments feel small while they are happening, yet they stay with us in a way that is impossible to forget: a look, a laugh, a song, or the comfort of simply being close.

With you, it is so easy to appreciate the little things. They do not need to be perfect or extraordinary. The softest memories are often the simplest ones, and I love knowing that they are ours.

These photographs and this song hold only a small part of what I feel. Still, I hope they remind you that every shared moment has a place in my heart.

This is with love, straight from me to you; simply, it is my heart.

♥
