A LETTER / FOR DAXIAN

마음을 담아, Daxian에게.

For the moments
that stay.

Daxian,

Some moments seem small when they happen. Later, they become the ones that are impossible to forget—a look, a laugh, a song, or simply sharing a little time together.

You made it easy to appreciate the little things. Nothing had to be perfect. Even the most ordinary moments felt worth keeping, simply because they carried something of you.

Maybe some feelings are not meant to become a life we can hold. Even so, what I felt was real to me, and these photographs, little things, and this song still carry a piece of it.

This is with love, straight from me to you; simply, it is my heart.

♥

avec tout mon cœurjust for you
